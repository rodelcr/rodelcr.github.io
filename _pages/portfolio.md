As an Osage and Puerto Rican scientist, I combine my training and scholarship in Astrophysics and Archaeology, as well as my artistic practice in visual arts, to create a future where Indigenous people probe the mysteries of the universe while knowing our scientific legacy.

My artistic practice highlights the environments and beings that shaped my ancestors, while critiquing the decontextualized and sterilized naturalism of Western artistic tradition like that of the Hudson River School and work from photographers like Ansel Adams and Edward Curtis.
